**HydroDrop — new open-source QGIS plugin**

Quick question on site planning: *If I pour 5,000 m³ of water here, where does it pond — and where does it spill?*

I released **HydroDrop** for QGIS: click a DEM, set a volume, get depth maps, multi-drop sessions, and engineering exports (GeoTIFF, GeoPackage, CSV).

Built for civil, mining, and environmental workflows where you need a fast what-if on terrain — not a full rainfall model.

• QGIS 3.28+ and 4.x  
• Open source (MIT)  
• github.com/ThermoTrack/HydroDrop  

If you work with DEMs and bunds/stockpiles/stormwater ponds, I'd welcome feedback.
